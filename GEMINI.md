# TaoFind Gemini CLI Extension

Connects Gemini CLI to **TaoFind** (`https://taofind.com/api/mcp`), providing stateless MCP tools for:
- 1688 / Taobao / Weidian link cleaning and privacy desensitization
- IATA volumetric haul shipping calculator (5000 vs 6000 divisor for air and sea lines)
- China reverse shopping proxy agent fee and foreign exchange (FX) markup benchmarks (Superbuy, Mulebuy, CSSbuy)
- Chinese clothing and shoe size conversion to US / EU standards

Website: https://taofind.com
